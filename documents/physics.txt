Physics is the foundational science exploring the fundamental laws of nature, focusing on the study of matter, energy, and their interactions through space and time. It spans from the subatomic level (quantum mechanics) to the entire universe (general relativity), aiming to understand natural phenomena from the smallest particles to massive galaxies.The study of optical properties in composite materials is a fundamental area of solid-state physics. When analyzing zinc oxide doped polymer films, researchers measure how light interacts with the material at a microscopic level. The transmission, absorption, and reflection of electromagnetic waves provide crucial data about the energy band gap and structural integrity of the composite. These physical measurements are strictly governed by quantum mechanics and are essential for developing advanced optoelectronic devices.
