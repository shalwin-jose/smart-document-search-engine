Chemistry is the central science studying the composition, structure, properties, and behavior of matter, as well as the changes it undergoes during chemical reactions. It investigates how atoms, molecules, and ions interact to form new substances, impacting everything from daily life to advanced materials. Major branches include organic, inorganic, and physical chemistry.The synthesis of polymer blends requires precise control over molecular bonding and chemical reactions. Introducing metallic compounds into a polymer matrix fundamentally alters its molecular structure. Chemists utilize spectroscopic analysis to observe how these covalent and ionic interactions modify the material's overall stability and reactivity. Understanding these chemical pathways allows for the creation of customized composite films with highly specialized synthetic properties and unique molecular compositions.
